Why decoding sometimes fails

      
A Base64 string must be made up only of A–Z, a–z, 0–9, +, /, and end with the correct amount of = padding (the total length must be a multiple of 4). Copy-pasting from an email client or chat app can silently strip trailing = characters or introduce line breaks — both will cause a decode error here.
