What's a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file stored in your browser by a website you visit. Cookies are commonly used to remember preferences, keep you signed in, or track behavior across visits for analytics or advertising purposes.

Cookies on DevFixTools

DevFixTools itself does not set first-party tracking cookies for its tools. Every tool on this site — the JSON formatter, JWT decoder, Base64 and URL encoders/decoders, and the rest — runs entirely client-side in your browser and does not depend on cookies to function.

Third-party advertising cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to display ads. Google and its ad-tech partners may set third-party cookies in your browser for ad personalization and frequency capping (limiting how many times you see the same ad).

You can opt out of personalized advertising via Google Ads Settings, and read Google's own cookie policy at policies.google.com/technologies/cookies.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies at any time through your browser's settings. Doing so may reduce the relevance of ads you see on this and other sites, but it will not affect the functionality of any tool on DevFixTools, since none of them depend on cookies to work.
