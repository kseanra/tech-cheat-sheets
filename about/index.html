About DevFixTools

DevFixTools is a free collection of client-side developer tools, practical guides, and error-solution pages — built for the moments when you just need to format some JSON, decode a JWT, or figure out why a command is throwing a cryptic error, without wading through ads and life stories to get there.

Every tool on this site runs entirely in your browser. Whatever you paste into the JSON formatter, the JWT decoder, or any other tool is processed locally with standard browser APIs — it is never uploaded to a server, never logged, and never seen by us.

There's no account to create and no login required to use anything here. The site is supported by Google AdSense advertising, which is what lets it stay free and open without requiring sign-ups or subscriptions.

Have a suggestion for a tool or spotted something wrong on an error page? Get in touch via the contact page.
